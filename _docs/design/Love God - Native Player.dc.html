On the system Now Playing stack
Don't rebuild the player — back it with Apple's audio infrastructure. You design two things: the in-app player and the artwork + metadata. Everything else — lock screen, Control Center, CarPlay, Apple Watch, background playback — is system-drawn from that same metadata, which is exactly why it stays stable.
Keeps playing when closed
Background Modes → Audio, plus an AVAudioSession set to .playback and activated on play. One AVPlayer on the stitched daily MP3 — a single file means rock-solid seeking everywhere.
Lock · Control Center · for free
Publish title, subtitle, artwork, duration & elapsed to MPNowPlayingInfoCenter; wire play / pause / skip ±15 / scrub on MPRemoteCommandCenter. You never custom-draw transport on these surfaces, so there's nothing to break.
CarPlay & Watch · zero extra UI
CarPlay renders your metadata in the system CPNowPlayingTemplate; the Watch Now Playing app mirrors the active session. Liturgy steps ride along as chapter markers, so "skip step" works on every surface.
